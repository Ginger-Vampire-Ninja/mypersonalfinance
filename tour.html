Cashflow Forecasting

Know exactly where you stand, months from now

Finaura builds a rolling 12–24 month cashflow table automatically. Every recurring payment, one-off transaction, credit card repayment and loan instalment is factored in — no manual number-crunching required.

  • Month-by-month income vs. expenditure breakdown
  • Running balance so you can spot a tight month early
  • Colour-coded net cashflow — green for surplus, red for deficit
  • Hover any cell for a full breakdown of what's included

Recurring Transactions

Set it once, tracked forever

Add anything that repeats — salary, rent, gym membership, subscriptions — and Finaura factors it into every future month automatically. You can edit or cancel at any time.

  • Weekly, fortnightly, monthly, quarterly or annual frequency
  • Irregular months — e.g. school-term costs that skip summer
  • Monthly equivalent shown for easy comparison across frequencies
  • Upcoming 60-day timeline so you always know what's coming

Credit Card Tracker

All your cards in one place

Add every card you hold, along with its balance, APR and any promotional 0% period. Finaura tracks minimum repayments and projects how your balances will change month by month.

  • Credit limit utilisation bar — green, amber or red at a glance
  • 0% interest-free deal tracker with days remaining
  • Log charges and payments against each card individually
  • Built-in payoff calculator: see how long minimum payments take vs. a fixed plan

One-off Transactions

Log anything that doesn't repeat

Holiday flights, a bonus payment, an unexpected car repair — add it once with a date and Finaura pulls it into the right month of your forecast automatically. Past entries build up your spending history.

  • Future-dated entries appear in the cashflow forecast
  • Past entries kept as a historical record
  • Income and expense in the same view, filterable by type
  • Export all transactions to CSV with one click

Loan Management

Keep loans out of your blind spot

Add your mortgage, personal loans or car finance and Finaura tracks repayments automatically. Monthly instalments flow straight into your cashflow forecast — so your projected balance always reflects what you actually owe.

  • Track any loan with a start date, end date and repayment amount
  • Repayments fold automatically into the cashflow forecast
  • Upcoming payments visible in the 60-day timeline
  • View remaining term at a glance
