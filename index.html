ℹ️ How interest-free deals work in your forecast

During the deal period, interest is only charged on any card balance above the interest-free amount. For example, if your deal covers £2,000 and your card balance is £2,400, interest is only charged on £400. If the balance is at or below £2,000, no interest is charged at all.

When the deal end date passes, your full card balance reverts to the standard APR. If you have multiple active deals on the same card, the amounts are combined.
